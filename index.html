How I think

Good products feel inevitable in hindsight.

01

Start with the tension

Find the real tradeoff underneath the request. The best product opportunities usually live there.

02

Make the first version legible

A prototype should make the idea easier to discuss — not pretend the hard questions are already solved.

03

Ship the learning

Outcomes are signals. Keep the loop open, share what changed, and let the product evolve with its users.
